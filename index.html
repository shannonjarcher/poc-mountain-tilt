Scroll

Five people, one rope, and the hour before the valley wakes. Nothing up here is in a hurry — not the light, not the snow coming off the summit, not us.

Leg one

The dark approach

The trail leaves the hut through dwarf pine and climbs steadily on switchbacks. You walk inside a small circle of lamplight, and the only measure of progress is the cold getting sharper.

Leg two

Gaining the snowfield

At the saddle the ground falls away on both sides and the twin peaks appear all at once, blue on white. Wind lifts the loose snow off the summit in a long, slow plume.

Leg three

First light

The summit catches it first, a thin wash that widens and slides down the face. For a few minutes everything is the same colour, and nobody says anything.

Leg four

The long way down

By the time the sun is fully up the mountain is just a mountain again. You take the east spur, knees complaining, already planning the next one.
